## Counts

- **PSI sales, Greater Sydney, last 12 months: not counted.** No PSI file could be fetched (Cloudflare challenge on both portal hosts). `showhaus/data/sales-sample.json` was therefore **not written**: the brief made it conditional on a real parse plus geocode, and synthetic sales would break the truth rules.
- **Geocode hit rate (GAT): 17/20 = 85%**. The 20 real Sydney street addresses came from EPA records, because PSI addresses were unavailable. 15/20 were exact-string hits, plus 1 after apostrophe removal and 1 via a unique range-start match. Median offset from EPA's own coordinate was 4 m (max 351 m, a large industrial site).
- Rental bond: 26,612 September 2026 lodgements, 18,381 in the target postcodes.
- School catchments: 1,657 primary polygons plus secondary and future files (2026 enrolment year).

## Parser: `scripts/psi-parse.mjs`

Node 22, no dependencies. It reads `.DAT` files, weekly or yearly `.zip` files (including nested zips, with a built-in ZIP reader) and directories. It parses B records with joined C records per the 2001–current spec, deduplicates by `propertyId|dealingNumber` (the latest file wins), filters to postcodes 2000–2234, 2555–2574 and 2745–2770 within a 12-month contract-date window, optionally geocodes against GAT with a cache, and writes `{generatedAt, window, sources, notes, counts, fields, rows}` with rows as arrays capped at 2 MB. A missing price, area or coordinate stays `null`.

```
node scripts/psi-parse.mjs --self-test
node scripts/psi-parse.mjs ~/Downloads/psi/ --asof 2026-11-08 \
  --suburbs KENSINGTON,RANDWICK,COOGEE,MASCOT,ARNCLIFFE,"WOLLI CREEK",ZETLAND,RHODES,PARRAMATTA \
  --geocode --cache /tmp/gat-cache.json --out showhaus/data/sales-sample.json
```

## Decisions needed before SHOWHAUS ships sold prices

1. **PSI commercial licence** from Valuer General NSW (contact listed on nsw.gov.au: vnswprivacy@dpie.nsw.gov.au). Without it, sold prices cannot appear in a commercial product, and the CC BY-NC-ND licence also blocks derived maps.
2. **Obtaining the files**: a human downloads them in a browser (Cloudflare blocks automation), or the PSI licence provides a feed.
3. **Sydney Airport ANEF 2039 geometry**: request it from Randwick or Bayside Council or Sydney Airport together with licence terms.
4. **Domain API**: register with a company email, then read the actual plan and quota and the T&Cs on listing storage and display.
